Playtest checklist

  1. Can you identify your allies and the approach immediately?
  2. Tap a unit, preview movement, and inspect terrain near a chokepoint.
  3. Compare Overview with the normal view. Can you return to the action easily?
  4. Move, open Roster, then close it. Check for camera jumps or hidden units.
  5. Try the compact phone size. Check target selection and edge panning.
