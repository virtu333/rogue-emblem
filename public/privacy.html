Rogue Dawn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Rogue Dawn is a single-player tactical game. This policy covers the Rogue Dawn apps for Android and iOS and the web version at emblem-rogue.netlify.app.

The Android and iOS apps

The apps do not collect, store on a server, or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The game runs entirely on your device: your saves and settings are kept in the app's own storage on the device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud), if you have it turned on, may include them, as it does for other apps. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

The web version

In a browser the game also keeps saves and settings in your browser's storage on your device. If a version of the game offers an optional account for cloud saves, it stores your username, a password managed by the sign-in service (Supabase), and your saves and settings, only to restore them on your other devices. The website's host (Netlify) keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on Rogue Dawn's Google Play or App Store listing.
