# UX polish pass (iPhone landscape playtest) — 2026-09-25

Driven by a phone playtest at ~844×390 (device ~852×393 / 932×430). Every change was
verified at 844×390, 667×375, ~1000×460 and desktop 1280×800. Screenshots:
`docs/art-direction/ux-polish/`.

Constraints held throughout: presentation only (no RNG in presentation, no save-format
changes beyond read-only summary fields), design tokens only (`uiPalette.json` →
`uiTokens.css`; DOM depths through `--re-depth-*`), Ink & Ember type roles (Cinzel for
ceremonial words, Press Start 2P kickers, body face for reading), BattleScene edits kept
thin (new behaviour lives in controllers/modules).

## 1. Tutorial: exits, coach, clearer lessons

- **Always a way out.** `TutorialCoach` (persistent, non-modal objective line over the map)
  carries **Leave** (aria "Leave tutorial") and **Skip step**. The pause menu (phone and
  desktop share the DOM pause) gains **Leave Tutorial** and, for a player with no saves,
  **Start First Run**. Both confirm first ("Leave the tutorial? Nothing from this practice
  battle is kept…"); Cancel is the focused default. Field notes inside the tutorial offer
  Continue + Leave tutorial.
- **Start First Run** records the tutorial as skipped, returns to Title with
  `autoAction: 'newGame'`, which runs the first-run fast path after the router cooldown.
  Victory offers *Start first run* / *Back to title* to a fresh player.
- **Teach by doing.** The Welcome / "tap a blue unit" / "tap the highlighted Fort" walls
  are gone when the DOM host exists: the coach states one goal at a time
  (`tutorialCoachModel.js`: select → move → fight → win, with protect/heal branches) and
  gate violations become a coach nudge instead of a blocking note. The XP lesson is a
  passing nudge. Canvas-only builds keep the old notes.
- **Copy.** Fort lesson names the bonuses of the tile reached; the casualty note now says a
  fallen unit is gone for good (Church can revive for gold) and that only the commander's
  fall loses the battle/run; the Vision intro explains Rewind in one breath.

## 2. Explain affordance (ⓘ + press-and-hold) replaces "About …" buttons

`src/ui/infoAffordance.js`: `attachInfo(card, …)` adds a compact ⓘ button (44px hit area,
aria-label "About <topic>", keyboard-focusable), a hover/focus-visible preview (popover in
the top layer), and a touch/pen press-and-hold on the whole card (450ms, move slop, swallows
the click after a hold; reduced motion drops the progress fill). A one-time tip teaches the
gesture on coarse pointers (`emblem_rogue_tip_hold_info`). Used by the roster (Combat
baseline, Class mastery, Weapon arts, attribute grid) and reward mastery notices.
Roster unit cards: class and HP on their own lines (class ellipsizes, HP as a bar + small
numbers); "Traits" is a proper section kicker; the content scroller fades at the edge;
Temperament reads as flavor (dashed, italic).

## 3. Danger reads at a glance

- `DangerZoneOverlay` draws crimson fill **plus** a bright outer edge and a world-aligned
  hatch, stepping in intensity with the number of enemies that reach a tile
  (fill α 0.20/0.30/0.40, hatch 0.60/0.72/0.80). Tokens `threatFill/threatEdge/threatInk/
  threatStatus`. Variants: global (hatched), pinned (denser fill, no hatch), focus (parchment
  edge for one enemy). Baked into RenderTextures (Graphics replay was starving timers).
- Idle enemy inspection outlines that enemy's reach (`InputController._showInspectedThreat`).
- The rail's **Danger** toggle lives in a fixed dock above the bottom tools: never inside
  the scroll region, swatch + "N in reach · hold to pin" state line, long-press pins.

## 4. Battle rail

- One bounded scroll region between the fixed header and the fixed dock/tools. Overflow is
  cued, not sliced: soft top/bottom fades and a "more ▾" / "more ▴" cue. Scroll resets on
  every state/menu change so the primary action is first and visible; focus is kept in view.
- Header compacts while a unit is selected/inspected; idle commands are one row
  (Inspect / Roster / Rewind) plus End turn. Bottom tools are four equal glyph cells.
- Objective line keeps its 24px row but takes taps across 44px; forecast "How to read"
  and shop "Compare with your roster" are full-height rows; forecast rows and shop copy
  fade at the edge.

## 5. Locate ready units

`UnitLocator.js`: the end-turn prompt's **Show <name> · i of N** (and **[N]** on desktop)
brings the next ready unit into view only if it is not comfortably on screen, selects it
(move range, unit panel, cursor live) and draws gold corner brackets closing in plus a short
beam (static fade under reduced motion). Repeated use walks ready units in reading order.

## 6. Boss presence on the boss

The map-covering boss bar is gone. `BossPresenceController` draws a compact ornate bar in
world space on the boss (gilt frame, crimson fill, gold drain chunk, crest; ember frame +
halo + flame pip when enraged; spans the Entity footprint, wordless for the Entity). It
follows the boss's own bar (which steps aside by alpha), hides with fog, drains in place
on defeat and returns on rewind. Name / HP / enrage reading stays off-map: the phone rail's
Battle details and objective help (`summaryLine()`), and a danger-toned line under the
desktop objective plate. The intro card stays. Par now shows during the boss intro
(`renderTurnCounter` at creation).

## 7. Band cards: one framing rule

Every band card (boss intro, recruit/lord join, phase band, fate/victory bands, level-up
and rite frames) follows two rules:
- **Band geometry:** bands span the battlefield (`bandSpan(frame, mapRect, 520)` →
  `--ce-band-l/r/w`), never the letterbox beside it.
- **Bust framing:** `--ce-bust-cap = min(band width × 0.36, band height + half the space
  above/below − 10px)`; the wipe animation clips only horizontally so the bust breaks
  out above the band instead of being sliced by it.
Kickers never ellipsize (`fitKickers()` shrinks to fit, min 6px). The level-up and rite
quotes (UnitVoice, ≤ ~90 chars) read in full: a third line on the narrowest phone frame
rather than an ellipsis; the rite's text column reserves the corner button's row; sealed
beats read on one line on short frames.

## 8. Save select: the candle shrine

`SlotPickerView.js` + `slotCardModel.js` + `slotPicker.css`. Three candle cards over the
dimmed Hollow Sun (`mountKeyArtBackdrop`, same variant as the title) with a deterministic
ember drift. Each card: candle + "SLOT I" kicker + friendly saved time; commander PC-98
portrait; act kicker, region (Cinzel) and grade in the act's mood colour; the Loom thread of
acts; "Battle suspended at River Crossing" / "On the road · Battle, stage 3" / "Setting out
into the Border Marches"; companions; milestone seals; runs tally; one primary action
(Resume battle / Continue run / Enter Home Base / Begin first run / Choose version) and a
small corner ✕ that still confirms. An empty slot is "An unlit candle — Begin a new
chronicle" with **New run**, which begins the first run in that slot. Choosing kindles the
candle (skipped under reduced motion). Accessible names kept for routing contracts
("Select Slot N", "Delete Slot N"). All three fit without scrolling at 844×390 and 667×375.
`getSlotSummary` gains read-only presentation fields (roster detail, act id/count,
node, template, milestones); old saves still render.
