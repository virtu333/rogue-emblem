# Anime opening: GPT image prompts, batch 2

Style: **The Unwritten Page**. Batch 1 review notes are at the end of this file.

**Send each prompt exactly as written.** Every prompt below is complete and includes the
style text. Don't summarise it or send only the table. In batch 1 the shots that were
sent without their full text missed their key requirement.

Attach the named PC-98 portrait from `public/assets/portraits/pc98/192/` with each
prompt. Where the image and the portrait disagree, the portrait wins.

## Shared rules (already inside every prompt)

- **Figures:** fine sepia ink inside the figure, one thicker contour around the
  silhouette, pale transparent watercolour, worn cloth and leather.
- **Backgrounds:** large simple shapes and soft wash, no hatching or fine texture, and
  quieter than any figure.
- **Cut-outs:** flat pure `#00FF00` background, no ground shadow, the whole figure inside
  the frame with a margin all round.
- **Landscape images:** keep the subject inside the middle 864 px band. The game crops
  1536×1024 to 16:9.
- **Cast cards:** all at the same scale, all three-quarter view facing right (our side
  always moves right; the Empire moves left).
- Edric's hair is **dark chestnut brown, not red or auburn**.
