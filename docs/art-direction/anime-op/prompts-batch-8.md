# Cutscene art: GPT image prompts, batch 8 ("The Night Before")

Style: **The Unwritten Page** ([STYLE.md](STYLE.md)). These are for the quiet scene planned in
[CAMP.md](../../../tools/cutscene/unwritten/CAMP.md): Edric, Sera and Kira at a fire on the
night before the battle (bars 5-12 of the opening). The scene is built first with the art we
already have (the camp cut-outs and four clips made from them); these images replace its
stand-ins and add the close-ups it wants.

**Send each prompt exactly as written.** Pose sheets come as several poses of one figure in a
row, at the same scale, on one ground line: one image keeps the character consistent between
poses, and code cuts them apart.

Why these: Edric's camp drawings face left (his sword hand is on the far side from the fire
in our staging), so the scene flips them; a drawing that faces right removes the flip and
lets the Thread's gold rim fall on the right side of him. Sera's eye and Kira's hands are the
inserts a quiet scene lives on. The props sheet replaces tents built from code with painted
fronts. The torchbearer is background business for the wide shots.

## After generating

Drop the images into the local folder as before. The local session keys the pose sheets into
`cutouts/` and adds catalogue rows. Good sheets then go to MiniMax as first and last frames
(`motion/camp_jobs.json`): pose 1 to pose 3 for Edric's look, pose 1 to pose 4 for the rise.
