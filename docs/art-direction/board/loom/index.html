The Loom

Sera sees possible futures as gold threads. The campaign map becomes her loom: the route already walked is woven gold, the choices within reach glow, the futures past them are undyed thread, and roads the party has passed by fray apart. The act ends at the Hollow Sun. The graph comes from the game's real NodeMapGenerator (act 1, seed 6), so every node, lane and edge is one the game can produce.

THREAD STATES

  • Woven: a plied gold rope with no glow, for the route already walked. A gold knot marks each cleared node.
  • Within reach: a bright rope with a warm glow and moving glints. The selected thread gets a pale core.
  • Possible future: a single thin grey thread that fades out two rows past the party.
  • Frayed: a broken thread whose ends shorten into dashes. Used for roads that can no longer be reached.
  • Vision: gold dashes along every route to an inspected future node.
  • Fracture: a crimson break on a thread and medal for elite battles, and cracks in the Hollow Sun's corona.

PALETTE IN USE

Ink carries the whole frame. Gold is used only for the path and for actions.

Blood marks the Lieutenant's fractures and elite battles. Verdigris is used for HP and helpful map features. Steel is used for fog.

TYPE

Cinzel 700 is used only for the act title. Press Start 2P (8–10px) is used for short labels: node kinds, row numerals and buttons. Prose uses the system sans at 11–13px, per the UI cohesion plan.

Nodes use the approved weathered node sheet through the same atlas rectangles as NodeArt.js.
