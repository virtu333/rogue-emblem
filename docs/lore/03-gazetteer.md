# 3 · Gazetteer

The land the March crosses, west to east, and the lands around it.

The rule of this map is the [name gradient](01-cosmology.md#the-gradient).
The border still names things. Every day east, fewer things have names, until
the Seat, where nothing does. Place names in this file are for the team.
In-game text should use them **sparingly**: generously in Act I, rarely in
Act II, almost never past the fens.

The Wend rises from the Glass at the foot of the Hearth and runs **west**,
through the fens, the grain provinces and the Marches, past the Ford and out
of the realm. The March goes **east**, upstream. Soldiers say they are
"walking the river back to where it's dirty". "The levy took my brother
east. You're heading east."

Seen from high enough, and only the sky legions ever flew that high, the
Wend, the fens and the Hearth's ridges trace the outline of something curled
on its side. The Glass sits where its mouth would be and the Hearth where its
heart would be.

## Act I · Border Marches

**Grade:** Ember Dusk. **Old names:** the Marches, the Line, the West.
**Imperial name:** *the March*, the border administration under the Iron
Captain. (*CROSSING CLOSED BY ORDER OF THE MARCH.*)

Hill country, quarry country and farmland along the lower Wend. This is the
last part of the old kingdom that kept the Oath after the Unsworn Night, and
it burned for it six years ago. It is still the most *named* place in the
game: people here say which river, which ford and which smith.

### Places

- **Oathford (the Ford).** The crossing where the first king swore the Oath
  and the Last King died. The river runs slow here. The Gentle King's crown
  lies somewhere in the gravel **(III)**. The Iron Captain's garrison holds
  the crossing and a stone toll-house built on the old kingdom's toll stones.
  The warband's fire on the night before the March burns in a hollow on the
  hill above it. Home base is here.
- **Wendhall.** The Uncrowned's hall on a bluff above the Ford, burned in
  S 28: four walls, a fallen roof and a great fireplace with nothing in it.
  Its gate is still standing. Cael held it for five years.
- **The Cut.** The grey-stone quarries upriver, where the Hallow's stones were
  once cut. Varen's forge still burns at the quarry village. The levy took
  the quarrymen and "even the picks". Its smith is Old Brannoc.
- **Birchwick.** The border wardens' village in the birch woods south of
  the Wend. It burned the same week as Wendhall, families inside. Voss's
  ledger begins here. A warden's daughter plaited a charm "from birch and
  hair" in Birchwick, and the trees still know her knot.
- **The Line.** The wardens' chain of watchtowers along the western hills. The
  empire garrisons half; the others are empty, or held by someone the
  empire does not know about.
- **The barrows.** Grass mounds along the Wend, older than the Oath. People
  plough around them. Ploughmen turn up Varen blades and older things.
- **The hill clans' country.** South-west, steep and wooded. The border clans
  live here under their own law and, since spring S 28, under the empire's
  new terms instead of the Old Treaties.

### Who lives here

Quarrymen, yeomen who owed the crown a quiver a season, deep-wood hunting
families, wardens' widows, and the Iron Captain's garrison. The garrison is
made of former wardens, fed and paid and ashamed. The hill clans raid from
the south. Hagen's cart comes through every week.

**Music:** Ember Dusk, Border Marches, Open Ground, The Oath at the Ford. No
choir: "Act I stays human-sized."

## Act II · Old Kingdom Roads

**Grade:** Iron Rain. **Old names:** the grain country, the Valley. **Imperial
names:** the **First through Ninth Provinces**, each run by a governor from
the Seat.

The broad middle valley of the Wend: wheat, orchards, remount studs, and the
old kingdom's great roads, which "run straight and true. The tolls are the
empire's." This is the empire's pantry. "This province feeds the war economy
a cartload at a time." The partisans move between the empire's columns here;
the score gives them a signal, two notes and a leap, passed from hill to hill.

### Places

- **The royal roads.** Paved by the old kingdom, re-milestoned by the empire.
  Milestones count down to the Seat. Partisans scratch out the numbers so
  patrols get lost.
- **Toll-houses.** Every bridge and crossroads has one. Many are abandoned.
  One ledger's last entry reads *Soldiers, forty. Paid, none. Toll-keeper,
  departed.*
- **The crossroads.** Where the Dark Rider always seems to be. Travelers say
  he rides the same crossroads in every province, which should be impossible.
  **(II)** It is the same crossroads, in different threads.
- **The remount studs.** Horse country in the Sixth Province, where Rowan
  grew up. Every remount office posts *RIDERS WANTED... The old oath is no
  longer required.*
- **The hill forts.** Old-kingdom forts on the grain roads, "hastily
  rebuilt" as imperial depots. "The fort watches the roads that once carried
  grain to the capital."
- **Old castles.** Banner-houses of the valley, most now imperial garrisons,
  their halls "emptied for battle" (Great Hall), their corridors "built for
  defense, not retreat" (Corridor Siege).
- **The old capital.** At the eastern end of the Roads, where the Wend leaves
  the fens. A ruin since the Nine Days, with no name, because its name went
  with the kingdom's. The north wall still has **the Breach**. The east gate
  still stands, blackened. The great bridge where the twins fell is two
  arches in the river. "Grass grows through the floor where the old court
  once assembled" (Castle Ruins). Imperial pickets avoid it at night.
- **Dueling halls.** In the market towns. Each has a ring, a gallery and a
  wall of hung gloves.
- **The Imperial War College** is not here; it is in the Seat. But its
  doctrine is everywhere, and so are its graduates.

### Who lives here

Tenant farmers, toll-keepers, remount grooms, garrison soldiers who haven't
been paid since the thaw, governors (some fled; "the general stayed, and the
general is worse"), partisans, deserters with their uniforms turned inside
out, and the empire's officers: the Knight Commander's lances, the Archmage's
circle on its way to the fens, the Dark Rider at the crossroads.

**Music:** Iron Rain, Steel and Thread, Old Kingdom Roads.

## Act III · The Imperial Heartland (the fens and the Hallow)

**Grade:** Bleached Rite. **Old names:** the Stillwater, the fens, the sacred
ground, the Hallow. **Imperial name:** the **Heartland**, crown demesne,
administered directly from the Seat and closed to civilians since S 25.

The empire calls the fens its heartland because they are the old kingdom's
heart: the Hallow sits at their far end. The circle has been feeding the
Hallow's first names into the dark since S 29, and the fens show it: black
still water, reeds bleached white, the dead standing up, light "too bright to
be kind". Choir voices enter the score here, "with the rite".

### Places

- **The causeways.** Stone roads on piles across the fen, built by the old
  kingdom, mostly sunk. The last dry one leads to the Hallow.
- **The Lieutenant's Keep** (*Dark Champion Keep*). A castle on the causeway at
  the fen's edge that "commands the last road into the imperial heartland".
  It was a chapter-house guesthouse before S 12. The Lieutenant grew up in
  it. His champions hold it for him.
- **Drowned villages.** Reed-islands whose names have faded with the water.
  Some still have one lit chimney.
- **The Stillwater.** The open fen, flat and silent, where "the frogs have
  gone quiet" and something big breathes under the water.
- **The Hallow.** A ring of standing stones on a rise at the fen's east end,
  grey stone from the Cut in the repairs and something older in the
  originals. The originals are the first-named dragons, lying down. Some
  have begun to move.
- **The Glass.** The black mere inside the ring. Perfectly still, and a
  perfect mirror. The Wend rises from it. Its far side is the Deep.
- **The chapter-house.** A reed-thatched hall on stilts at the water's edge,
  home of the seer order for longer than the kingdom existed. Burned in S 30.
  Its bells still hang and still ring on their own: "twice, then silence. Then
  twice."
- **The berserk school.** A stockade at the fens' western edge where Wat's
  pupils learn the red, and where the ones who learn it too well are chained
  at night.
- **The shrine posts.** Prayer boards on poles along the causeways, gone blank.
  "The paint didn't fade. The words did."

the berserk school, the Lieutenant's champions and their garrisons,
revenants, and the first woken dragons, which are hungry. Hagen poles a
barge through the reeds, humming a hymn off-key.

**Music:** Bleached Rite, Against the Rite, Petals on the Fen ("this ground
has its own dead"), The Mire.

## Act IV · The Ashen Frontier (the Hearth and the capital)

**Grade:** Ashfall; Rime Night on the snow. **Old name:** the Hearth, the Warm
Mountain. **Imperial names:** the Seat (the city), the Hearth Marches (the
uplands).

East of the fens the land climbs into the **Hearth**: a broad volcanic massif
whose stone is warm all year, with the capital on its western shoulder and
high frozen passes behind it. "Frontier" is the empire's word, because
beyond the passes lie the peak clans and the Dry Country. The seers had
another meaning: this is the frontier between the named world and the Deep.

The Great Feeding has made the mountain restless. The Hearth has been
smoking since S 29 and ash falls on the city. The corona is so thin here that
day is night. **True night begins here.**

### Places

- **The capital.** A city that spilled down the Hearth's shoulder from the
  palace-fortress in thirty-four years. Its streets are "swept clean. Nobody is
  left to dirty them", because the Great Feeding has been reading the city's
  own Roll. The curfew has crept from dusk to noon. The heraldry office is
  ash. There is a cathedral with no roof and one priest.
- **The palace-fortress.** The Emperor's residence and the throne room, whose
  doors "have not been closed in a month". Household knights in parade plate
  "reforged for war". The walls "were raised to keep something in".
- **The Imperial War College.** Beside the palace. Its lecture halls teach the
  *Doctrine of Lances*.
- **The Caldera.** The Hearth's crater, "a furnace older than the empire",
  ringed by a road the last surveyors marked before the lava cut it.
- **Eruption Point.** The watchtower "where the mountain last broke open",
  in the Storm-Year, O 519.
- **The high passes.** Caravan routes north-east to the peak clans, snowed shut
  since the first winter of the Great Feeding. "Caravans once crossed this
  pass before the first winter snows."
- **The glacier fortresses.** Imperial bastions guarding the passes, their
  gates sealed by ice.

## The Imperial Seat and the Deep (the final act)

**Grade:** the Throne (the Lieutenant) or the Deep (the Entity).

Below the palace a stair goes down into the mountain's roots. It is the old
kings' coronation stair, and none of them came back up it with a name. At the
bottom is **the Hearthstone**: a hall of warm stone that beats. The old kings
had a throne cut there to sit on while they spent themselves. The ritual has
worn channels into its floor. This is the **Eldritch Sanctum**: "The ritual
has worn channels into stone that once held a throne."

The Sanctum is also **the far side of the Glass**. Under the mountain, the
Hearthstone and the Glass are two openings onto the same body.

- **The stair.** Carved, very old: *WE CAME THIS FAR ALSO.* Newer, in a seer's
  hand: *IT REMEMBERS YOU. BE QUICKER.* Newest, fresh: *NOT THIS TIME.*
- **The landing.** Someone has been camped here a long while. Their fire is
  still lit. Hagen has set up shop on the last stair.
- **The last door.** Carved above it: *WHAT SLEEPS HERE WAS NOT MEANT TO BE FED.*
- **The Deep.** What lies past the Sanctum on Lunatic: the Sleeper itself,
  where the air does not move and time does not pass.

The Lieutenant waits in the Sanctum because he has always lived on the far
side of the Glass. On Normal the warband comes to him from below, through the
Glass. On Lunatic it comes down the stair after the Emperor falls, and he is
already gone ([08 § Routes](08-reconciliation.md#the-three-routes)).

**Music:** Every Future You Could Reach (the Lieutenant); · · · and All of Us
Across (the Entity); The Loom: Unlight.
