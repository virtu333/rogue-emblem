# Canon in two pages

Draft. See `README.md` for what the marks mean.


## 1. The world in eight lines

1. Something sleeps under the land. It is older than the ground consecrated to keep it asleep, and it does not hate. **[in game]**
2. It eats time that was never lived. **[new, sealed as a plain statement]**
3. The old kings kept it quiet. Each went down a stair under the stones once, and came back older. **[stair in game; the price is new]**
4. A generation ago a general found the debt could be paid with other people's years. He became the Emperor. He "traded the empire's future for the ritual's promise". **[in game, sharpened]**
5. His court circle runs the ritual and keeps the ledger. People it draws on become the Hollowed. **[circle and Hollowed in game; the link is new]**
6. A few people can see the threads of what may happen and pull one back. They are seers. The court takes them young. **[seers in game; taken young is new]**
7. Sera is a seer who ran. The Lieutenant is the one who stayed. **[in game, sharpened]**
8. Every time a seer says "again", the morning thrown away goes down the stair. **[sealed]**

## 3. The road

| Act | Region (in game) | What it is in the story |
|---|---|---|
| I | Border Marches | Edric's home. Imperial garrisons and the border clans, both left behind by their own sides. |
| II | Old Kingdom Roads | The country the Empire was built on top of. The circle's couriers and scholars work here. |
| III | Imperial Heartland | Where the Empire shows what it is proud of: duelling halls, doctrine, the old capital's breach. |
| IV | Ashen Frontier | The capital and the ground around it, emptied by the levy. Curfew at noon. The paving hums. |
| V | The Imperial Seat, then below | The Old Kings' Stair, the Mortuary Column, the Vigil, the Glass, and the sleeper. |

Rule kept from the old bible: **names get more frequent the further east the army goes.** Act I names almost nothing. By Act IV there are street names, regiments and notices. Below the throne there are no names at all.

## 4. People

**The army.** Edric, lord of the Marches, who counts his people. Sera, the seer. Kira, the tactician. Voss, the ranger who keeps a ledger of his own. Rowan, the lucky horseman. Astrid, who flew alone. Cael, the shield. Gaspar, Edric's father's old knight, and Tamsin, an archer from Harrow's Crossing, in the first thread. **[all in game; bios are not written yet]**

**The Empire's officers** go by rank. The court's ledger lists them by what they owe. Killing one gives the save his name and a paragraph of who he was (the old bible's histories for the Iron Captain, Warchief, Knight Commander, Archmage, Dark Rider, Blade Lord, Iron Wall and Berserker King carry over). **[titles in game; the reason and the reveal are new]**

**The Emperor** counts years the way Edric counts people. He believes the arithmetic still runs in his favour. **[in game]**

**The Lieutenant** is the Emperor's seer. He has watched every future Sera walks "from the far side of the glass". He is from the Marches, and Tamsin was named for him. He holds the last door because where the weaving stops, so does he. **[first two in game; the rest new, the last sealed]**

**The sleeper** never speaks. It hums. It remembers. **[in game]**

## 5. Words

- **Thread.** One version of the morning. A run. **[in game]**
- **Hollow Sun.** The sky when the future is being drawn down. It has happened before. Some people worship it. **[three uses in game, one meaning new]**
- **The Eclipse.** How far the sun has gone on this thread. **[in game]**
- **Court circle.** The Emperor's ritual college. **[in game]**
- **The Rite.** The words the Voices speak to wake the sleeper. Fewer words, a weaker waking. **[in game]**
- **The Hollowed.** People whose remaining years were taken. They answer the roll and little else. **[in game; the cause is new]**
- **The Glass.** The Lieutenant's hall of mirrors, each a little late. **[in game]**
- **Echo.** The sleeper has met a veteran before, and meets them harder. **[mechanic in game; meaning new]**
- **Knots.** Snags in a thread woven too many times. **[mechanic in game; meaning new]**
- **Rogue dawn.** A morning nobody foresaw and nobody can take back. **[new]**

## 6. How it is told

- Show the world through objects and overheard lines, as the game does now. No narrator.
- Item lore should carry the world a little more than it does. About one line in ten should touch the old kingdom, the Empire, the circle or a place.
- Edric counts people. Only the Emperor counts years. Nobody else should pick up either habit.
- Nothing before the special ending says that weaving feeds the sleeper. Lines may come close: Sera can doubt, the Lieutenant can warn, the carving can say BE QUICKER.

## 7. Sealed until the special ending

- What the sleeper eats, said plainly.
- That Sera has fed it more than the court has.
- Why the Lieutenant holds the door.
- The choice at the knot: weave again, or let it be morning.
