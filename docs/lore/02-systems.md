# What the systems are

Draft. See `README.md` for what the marks mean.

Each game system has a place in the fiction, or should. This file lists them so that new text about a system says the same thing every time. The rule: a system's fiction must never promise a rule the game does not have.

"Fiction today" is what the game's own text already says. "Reading" is what this draft adds on the un-lived time direction, and is **[new]** throughout unless marked.

## 1. Time and the thread

| System | Fiction today | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **A run** | A thread. "Not this thread." "Again, from the morning I reached you." | One version of the morning Sera reached Edric. |
| **The run ends when Edric falls** | His last words, then the game over. Other lords can fall and the run goes on. | The thread is tied to the morning she reached *him*. Without him there is nothing to pull. |
| **Vision (rewinds in battle)** | Sera's sight. Charges are spent to take an action back. | A small "again". Each one is a morning thrown away. **[sealed: that it feeds the sleeper]** |
| **Starting another run** | Run-start lines that remember the last run's losses. | A large "again". |
| **Branching Threads (rerolls)** | The name already says it. | Sera looks down a second branch before choosing. |
| **Redraw road** | Bought with Valor from the route map's menu. | The same, for the road ahead. |
| **The prologue** | The first thread: the quarry road, the gate, the sun going hollow. | The one morning that really happened. Every run after is her trying it again. |

## 3. The court and what it makes

| System | Fiction today | Reading |
|---|---|---|

| **The Rite and the four Voices** | Calling, Binding, Feeding, Waking. "The stone is laid." "Take the roll." | The call that tells the sleeper its meal is laid. See `01-roads.md` section 4. |

| **Necromancers and Skeletons** | "Third Mortuary Company: 400. Returned: none. They are still marching." | The court does not let the dead stop serving. A Necromancer is a clerk of the Mortuary Column sent into the field. |
| **Zombies and Revenants** | Rise again unless finished properly. | The same debt, collected less carefully. |
| **Revival Stones** | A boss refills a health bar when a stone breaks. | A year held in reserve, spent when the officer would otherwise die. The Emperor issues them. |
| **Futuresold (the Emperor's sword)** | "The Emperor bought it with years not yet lived. The debt passes to whoever holds it." | Exactly what it says. |
| **Affixes on foes** | Named traits on stronger enemies. | Marks of how long a soldier has been on the ledger. Keep this unsaid in play. |

## 4. The army

| System | Fiction today | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruits** | Voices built from class, temperament and trait. They address the commander by name. | People who chose a side on a morning that keeps happening. |
| **Marks** | Forge, Hunt, Ember, Veil, Road: a rare second gift on a recruit. | Leave unexplained. Some people are born with one. |
| **Deeds, epithets and Oaths** | Earned in battle and kept on the unit. | What a soldier did on this thread, which is the only one they remember. |
| **Fallen allies** | Farewell lines; a living lord answers; the next run's opening may name them. | The cost of a thread. Sera carries the names forward; nobody else can. |
| **Church: heal, revive, vows, cleanse** | Flavour lines; revival at a price. | Open. Reviving the dead inside one thread needs a reason that is not the court's. See section 7. |
| **Blessings and their prices** | Shrine cards with a boon and a cost. | Vows at roadside shrines older than the Empire. Every vow costs something; that is what makes it a vow. |
| **Colosseum** | Mercenaries, bouts, a crowd that goes home. | The Empire's entertainment, still running while the sky fails. |
| **Lingering Injury, Hunted, Sworn Enemy, Debt, Ill Omen** | Burdens taken from events and bargains. | What a choice on the road leaves behind. |

## 5. After a win

| System | Fiction today | Reading |

| **Knots (Heat)** | Opt-in challenges. "Sera's sight is frayed." "The road's foes have trained for you." | Snags in a thread woven too many times. The more she pulls, the more it knots. |
| **Run grade, achievements** | Bragging only. | None. Keep them outside the fiction. |

## 6. Things with their own small story

- **The Iron Guardian and the Sunken Mine.** An event's set piece. A thing the old kingdom built to keep a door shut, still doing it.
- **The Toll-House and the Toll Crossing.** Imperial toll posts on old-kingdom roads.
- **Epic weapons and boss trophies.** A trophy is what an officer leaves behind, and its lore is his story in one line (the Iron Captain's Unrelieved Line "held one road for nine years"). When "names returned" is built, the trophy and the name should arrive together.
- **Darkest Before Dawn.** A side mode at Home Base. Not part of the story; a tale told at the fire.

## 7. Gaps

These systems have no settled fiction, and text about them should stay vague until they do.

1. **Reviving the fallen at a church or the Ruins.** In a world where the villain's crime is spending other people's time, the heroes buying a life back needs care. One option: the church cannot raise the dead, only those "not yet counted", which is why it is limited per act and why the Ruins leave an injury.
2. **Why a merchant is everywhere.** The old draft had one merchant who remembers every thread. It fits this direction even better, but it is a character, and belongs in the people chapter.
3. **What Valor and Supply are.** Probably nothing. Leave them as numbers.
4. **Who else remembers.** Bosses half-remember ("the post keeps ending the same way"). Soldiers do ("Didn't we fight here already?"). Recruits should not, or the loop loses its loneliness. Proposed rule: the closer someone stands to the court's work, the more they remember.
