# 6 · Bestiary and Relics

What the warband fights and why it is like that; where the legendary arms
came from; and the stories behind the objects already in the game.

---

## Part one: the enemy

### The levy

Most of what the warband kills. Farmhands, quarrymen, toll-keepers' sons,
levied by province and entered in the Roll: *LEVY BY ORDER. AGAIN.* They
march to the imperial cadence, and the longer they march to it the quieter
they get.

- **They carry no light.** Their names are in the Roll in the Seat, not in
  them. At night your units glow and theirs do not.
- **They can come back to themselves.** A levy soldier who deserts, turns his
  uniform inside out and is called by name starts to glow again. Act II
  recruits are this.
- **Death.** "Enemies: crimson cinders to ash." The red is the Roll's ink
  burning off.

### Imperial regulars and elites

"Imperial elites. Not conscripts — true believers." The drill has worked on
them completely. They salute each other ("Bad sign. It means someone's in
charge"), march in step through bog and keep the loser's gloves. An **elite
company** (*Against the Standard*) is a regiment whose drill-master still
remembers the old kingdom's cadence and hates it.

### Officers, captains and affixes

A captain is an officer who has surrendered his name and been given rank in
exchange, and rank comes with gifts from the court circle. **Affixes** are
those gifts: pieces of other people's names, read into an officer to make him
more than one man.

| Affix | What it is |
|---|---|
| Regenerator | Wounds close because the officer's body is reading from someone else's shape. |
| Thorns | A crackle of names that are not his, resenting the contact. |
| Venomous, Corrosive | Fen-craft. Water from the Stillwater, where things forget to stay whole. |
| Rally | His rank-name is written above his men's in the Roll, and they obey it. |
| Waller | The ground answers him instead of the warband. |
| Teleporter | His thread is doubled. The Lieutenant has sealed one of his positions and left the other. |
| Shielded | A ward bought with a village's names. |
| Berserker | He studied at Wat's school. |
| Deathburst | When he dies, what was read into him comes out at once. |
| Anchored | The Roll holds him in place. |
| Haste | Borrowed legs. |

The Art Bible's rule, "affixes read as threads snapping", is canon: they are
where the Lieutenant has tampered with the weave.

### Hunters

The empire's bounty parties that close on a named recruit before the warband
can reach them: "Hunters are closing on Garrick." The Roll marks certain
people for collection: deserters, the unregistered, anyone whose name has
got loud. Hunters bring them in so their names can be read at the Hearth.
A hunter **Captain** carries the sealed list.

### Bandits

The village raiders are **the unrolled**. They are not imperial. When a village
is fed, the survivors who were away that day come home to houses nobody
remembers and neighbors who do not know them. Some go to the chaplains. Some
go to the hills and take what they need from villages that still have names.
They hate the named more than they hate the empire. *Bells Over the Village*:
the bell calls and "a few, then many, then nobody" answer.

### Zombies and revenants

The fens' dead: bodies whose names were **fed**, so that nothing calls them
home. They are one kind at two ages, as the class chain says.

- **Zombies** are the newly risen. They get up and wander. "Burn the dead, or
  give them to the light." Light works because it calls a name back; if there
  is no name left to call, burn.
- **Revenants** are zombies the fens have kept long enough. They have started
  taking the living's names in small pieces to rebuild a shape of their own,
  and they have stopped shambling. "It remembers the axe, the sword, and the
  hunger." What fills behind their ribs when they wound you is your name. The
  dead levy make the most orderly revenants: the drill is the last thing to
  go. The Emperor's last guardians are revenants in the stacked standards of
  conquered realms, fed first and fattened longest.

"That one doesn't sleep. I've watched it not sleep."

### Dragons, "the woken"

The Dawn's first-named. They lay down around the Glass on the night of the
Starfall with their bodies in the ground and their backs to the sky, and
over the ages what shows above the earth became the ring of the Hallow. They
"slept below the sacred ground"; the stones are their spines. They dream the lullaby, and the
peak clans say their dreams hatch wyverns.

Since S 29 the circle has been reading **their** names into the dark, the
oldest names in the world and the most nourishing. Each dragon whose name is
read wakes. They wake in the wrong order, because the lullaby that laid them
down was a sequence and the circle reads the Roll by number. "The first ones
up are hungry." A woken dragon has lost its name and is looking for it. That
is why they go for the warband: named things smell like what it lost.

- **Dragon breath** is the Old Tongue, spoken by the only throats that ever
  said it first. Fire Breath is "the oldest word in the dragon tongue"; its
  one word is *fire*. Toxic Breath is a dragon trying to say a word it has
  forgotten. Ancient Breath "exhales from before the chronicles began".
- **The Dragon Lord** is the eldest of the woken, the first dragon the Dawn
  named. "It regards the war the way a mountain regards weather." It is not
  the warband's enemy. It is in the way.
- **Dracoshield** is "Genuine dragon scale!" Some of it is. Hagen sells the rest.

### Wyverns

Born in the peak clans' high nests "in winters when the stones of the Hallow
stir". Ridden by clan riders and sold to the empire, "three wyverns... consider
me the refund". Wyverns go restless near the fens and won't eat ("Something
stirs under the fens and my wyvern won't eat") because they can smell the
dreams they came from going wrong. "Wyverns remember."

### The Sleeper's manifestations

What the Entity brings to the field is not a weapon. It is the Stillness
moving, which should not happen.

- **Eldritch Grasp.** "It does not reach across the field. The field folds
  until it is already here." Distance is a kind of *after*, and near the
  Sleeper it fails.
- **Twisting Vortex.** "The stillness, moving. Geometry declines to describe it."
- **Its portrait** is a texture of eyes: everything it has swallowed, looking out.
- **Its death**: "violet-black motes pulled inward." It is pulling what it can
  back in as the choir pulls out.

## Part two: the legendary arms

Every Legend-tier weapon in `weapons.json`, with its history. Shipped lore is
quoted first. Nothing here changes it; this is what it refers to.

### Swords

**Brave Sword.** *"The chronicle credits its champion with one stroke at the
bridge. Two men fell."* The royal champion's blade at the end of the Twins'
War (O 338), which parted Rhun and Rhodri on the bridge of the old capital.
Its bearer was never named in the chronicle, at his own request. He did not
want to be remembered for it.

**Gemini.** *"Forged for twin princes who would share nothing, it learned to
strike for both."* Commissioned by the twins' mother so they would have to
share one sword. They took turns with it through seven years of war. It
strikes twice because it never learned which brother it belonged to.

**Ragnarok.** *"It held the gate when every wall behind it fell, and held it
through the night."* The royal gate-blade of the old capital's east gate.
**Cenwyn**, the queen's gate-warden, carried it through the Nine Days. It was
pulled from the rubble of the gate on the tenth morning by an imperial
sapper who knew what it was and deserted with it. It has belonged to people
who held doors ever since.

**Soulreaver.** *"Its bearers never tire in battle. They never sleep well
after, either."* A court circle blade, forged S 20 for officers of the
Heartland. It takes a little of each name it cuts. Its bearers sleep badly
because they dream other people's lives.

**Eldritch Grasp.** The Entity's. Not a sword. See above.

### Lances

**Brave Lance.** *"At the ford, the chronicle says, each rider struck twice and
the river ran slow."* The lance of the first king's champion at the fight in
the river on the day of the Oath (O 1). The oldest weapon in the kingdom's
chronicle. It belongs to the Ford the way the crown does.

**Gae Bolg.** *"Named for a hero who never started a fight, and never lost
one."* The Old Tongue name means roughly *the answer that comes back*. Its
first bearer was a warden of the Line in the fourth century who took the
dueling halls' first rule, *never strike first*, as a creed for a whole life.
It is strongest on the counter because it was made for a person who only
ever answered.

**Doomblade.** *"The chronicles name this lance once and never again."* The
great lance of the old kingdom, whose **name the Gentle King spent** at the
Hearthstone in O 601 to break the Grey Winter. The chronicle entry before
that year names it. Every entry after says *the lance*. That one entry is the
only place the name survives, which is how anyone knows to call it Doomblade.
Smiths who read the word aloud say it sounds like nothing, as if it had been
emptied. It still hits like it remembers.

### Axes

**Brave Axe.** *"Dug from a barrow holding one king and two sets of armor. It
swings for both."* From a barrow on the Wend, before the Oath. The barrow
king was buried with his armor and someone else's, and the chronicle has
never settled whose. Border folk say his shield-brother, who died the same
day, went into the mound with him. The axe hits twice for two men.

**Stormbreaker.** *"Borne down the clan peaks in the storm-year. Weather has
walked around it since."* The peak clans brought it down in O 519, the only
time they ever fought for a banner, and held the Hearth passes with it
through a winter of storms. It was left in the king's armory as a pledge and
lost at the Nine Days. The clans would very much like it back.

**Ruin.** *"It answers to no triangle, and to no name but the last one its
smith spoke."* A smith-saint's axe from the Cut. Its smith spoke a name into
it as she quenched it and then died. The name was her own. It ignores the
weapon triangle because it is only listening for her.

### Bows

**Brave Bow.** *"The chronicle has the arrow drawn once and loosed twice. The
chronicler was there."* The only Legend weapon whose record was written by an
eyewitness: the border chronicler Osgar of the Line, who also shot the bow.

**Starfall.** *"Strung the night the stars came down, it aims as if it
remembers where they fell."* Strung on the night of the Spending by a fenfolk
hunter who looked up. Older than the Oath, older than the barrows. Its range
is long because it is still aiming at where her change fell.

**Doublebow.** *"The watchtower hermit who strung it left one line behind:
company spoils the aim."* A warden who asked for the loneliest tower on the
Line and got it. The Line still calls that tower *the Hermit's*.

### Tomes and light

**Excalibur.** *"Wind, written down. Fliers who hear the page turn have already
begun to fall."* The academy's great wind-tome: the Old Tongue name of wind,
written out whole. The only complete copy was saved from the burning in S 13
by a student who carried it out under their shirt. The Art Bible calls its
element "a seer's wind".

**Bolting.** *"Three copies were written for the siege of the capital. One
remains. Stand back."* Written by the court circle for the Nine Days. Two
copies opened the Breach. The third was never used and sits in the court
circle's vault, where its scribes copy from it for imperial siege-casters.
The copies are worse, and hotter. Any Bolting the warband picks up is one of
them, or it is the one.

**Luce.** *"Written to end one thing under the sacred ground. The page says so.
The rest is ash."* The most dangerous book in the world, and the saddest.
Written by a seer of the order in the Storm-Year (O 519), after the first
thinning, as an attempt to **name the Sleeper**, to give the Stillness a name
and with it an ending. The seer burned before finishing. What survives is
the first page and the purpose, and it is enough to make holy light burn
anything dark. Whether a finished Luce would kill the Sleeper or wake it into
something with a future is a [deliberate mystery](01-cosmology.md#deliberate-mysteries).

**Twisting Vortex.** The Entity's. See above.

### Staves

**Fortify.** *"The last page of the healing rite: everyone, at once, and let the
cost be mine."* The temple's great healing rite, from the last page of the
healer's manual. It is the Oath's first line as a prayer. The healer's own
name bears the cost.

**Warp Staff.** *"Banned by the war colleges: marching is doctrine, not a
suggestion."* A seer's staff, made at the chapter-house from a piece of the
Glass's rim. It folds the weave for a moment. The war colleges banned it
because it made logistics meaningless and cadets lazy.

## Part three: storied objects

Accessories and consumables whose lore already tells a story, with what is
behind it. Most are small, which is right: the world is made of small
things.

| Object | Shipped lore | Behind it |
|---|---|---|
| **Goddess Icon** | "Thumbed blank by the dying. The goddess's name was not forgotten; it was spent." | Every soldier's pendant of the Hollow Sun. The dying thumb it for the name they cannot say. Thumbed blank, it looks like her statues. |
| **Forest Charm** | "Plaited by a warden's daughter from birch and hair. The trees know her knot." | **Tilde's**, Voss's daughter, from Birchwick. She made dozens and gave them away. Voss does not know any survived **(II)**. |
| **Mentor's Band** | "Old Hessa taught forty recruits in it. Thirty-one lived. She called it a good year." | The drill-mistress of the western Line. She trained Voss, Carrow and Tilde. |
| **Phalanx Band** | "Issued in pairs. If you find one alone, don't ask the quartermaster why." | Old-kingdom household knights wore paired bands with their shield-brothers. Most singles are the survivor's half. **Rowan's lance-master sold his in a lean year; its pair is on the Knight Commander's arm (II).** |
| **Phoenix Brooch** | "His wife pinned it on him at the gate. Twice he fell, and twice it heard her." | Revival by name: she said his name into it when she pinned it. It works as long as she is alive to have meant it. |
| **Barrier Ring** | "Inside the band, in a child's letters: NO FIRE ON PAPA." | A child's name-ward. It works, a little, because a child meant it completely. |
| **Soothing Stone** | "A river stone his mother warmed for his cot. It never quite cooled again." | A stone from the Wend. It was warmed at a hearth lit from the Hearth, so it holds a coal of the old kingdom's fire. |
| **Vampire's Bloodshard** | "Chipped from something dug up in the fens. It was still warm. It still is." | A chip of a woken dragon's heart-scale. It is trying to get its name back from whoever it cuts. |
| **Moontide Amulet** | "Tide-silver. Some nights it sings, some it sulks." | From the Saltmarch, before its lighthouses went dark. Coastal silver keeps time with the tide. |
| **Delphi Shield** | "Sky legion issue." | *Delphi* is the legions' old word for the high air above the pickets, where the corona is near. |
| **Mercury Sandals** | "Winged heels out of an old myth." | The myth is the Morning: the Dawn's messengers ran on air before the pegasi drew her. These are a forgery of those, and they work anyway. |
| **Seraph Robe** | "Temple sisters sewed it for a son they never had. It fits whoever needs it." | Sewn with names worked into the hem, all the children the sisters named at the shrine. |
| **Angelic Robe** | "Temple linen, blessed once and holding it still. Wear it under the mail." | Blessed once, by name, and the linen hasn't forgotten the name. |
| **Wrath Band** | "The berserk school's leash, worn backward." | Wat's graduates are chained at night. The band is that chain, and it loosens when you bleed. |
| **Counter Seal** / **Duelist's Glove** | "ONE AT A TIME, GENTLEMEN." | Dueling-hall rules. In the old kingdom a duel settled a name; in the empire it settles a wager. |
| **Bounty Hunter's Mark** | "The guild pays; the guild never asks whose." | Hunters' guild brand. Some of the heads it paid for were on the Roll's collection lists. |
| **Master Seal** | "Commission, name line blank." | An imperial officer's commission, **with the name line blank because the officer gives up his name to sign it**. A promotion bought by the empire's rules. The warband uses the paper and keeps the name. |
| **Spirit Dust** | "Ground from a chapel ward-stone." | Ward-stones hold a parish's names. Taken on the tongue, "the words come easier" because other people's names are helping. |
| **Elixir** | "The recipe died with the last temple apothecary." | She died in the Defacing (S 9). The recipe was in a shrine book the clerks took. |
| **Varen blades** | "Stamped VAREN by the guard, same as your father's." | See [the chronicle](02-chronicle.md#varen-o-404--471). |
| **Stormbreaker, Brave Axe, Ragnarok** | | See above. |
