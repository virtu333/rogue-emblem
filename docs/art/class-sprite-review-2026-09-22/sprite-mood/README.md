# Sprite mood experiment

Three separate built-in imagegen treatments of the same gray-haired veteran Archer. Exact prompts in catalog.json; source PNGs in sheets, game-sized textures in sprites. No production replacement.

## Direction
- A: retain compact proportions and upright stance; replace electric cobalt with moderately desaturated steel blue. Keep bright bow, sleeves and hair.
- B: restrained palette plus smaller head, stronger shoulders and an offset, battle-ready stance.
- C: restrained palette and mature proportions, drawing the bow with clear arm angles and compact weapon reach.

The aim is a more grounded tone without losing broad color masses, minimal shading, class silhouette or white weapon contrast. Whole-image darkening risks losing visibility on terrain and reduces room for acted-state tint. Use midtone cloth and selective lights instead.

## Method
Same Archer identity and reference for all samples. Same current infantry placement, foot anchor and map positions. Previous-candidate toggle shows the original sprite-first Archer in all three lower positions. Edric, Sera and Pegasus are unchanged anchors. Three generations are exploratory rather than perfectly controlled edits. Differences beyond requested variables may occur.

## Pose guidance
Active readiness can communicate class without animation. A full attack pose may look permanently mid-action and can increase width enough that fitting shrinks the body. Evaluate body size separately from weapon extent; a compact ready pose is likely more flexible as the default idle sprite. Reserve exaggerated attack motion for animations if needed.

## Limits
Static art probes in the actual game renderer, not gameplay tests. Coarse-pixel generation does not guarantee a uniform pixel grid. No acted/fog/animation validation claimed. User review of tone and legibility remains decisive.

## Observations
- A's muted cloth is closer to the restrained palette while the bow remains clear on grass and stone. Large head/eye proportions still contribute a cartoon tone.
- B and C converged on nearly the same aiming pose, retained stronger cobalt, and only partly followed the proportions request. These are documented generation deviations, not validated separate style directions.
- The aiming pose communicates Archer strongly through the horizontal arrow and open bow shape, but reads as an attack rather than an idle stance.
- Recommended next direction: A's palette, a genuinely compact ready posture, and smaller facial features while retaining coarse connected clusters. Avoid adding intricate costume detail as a shortcut to maturity.
- All three extracted without boundary clipping warnings. Map assessments are qualitative.
