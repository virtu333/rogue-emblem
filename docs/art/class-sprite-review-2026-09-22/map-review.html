Next art pass

  • Keep named lord identities fixed. Preserve each recruit identity through promotion.
  • Distribute black, dark brown, ash gray, sandy blond, auburn and occasional muted fantasy hair across classes. Do not default every pair to brown-haired man / red-haired woman.
  • Vary skin tones, age, hair silhouette, build and practical clothing while retaining clear class weapons and blue faction accents.
  • Map readability first: bows, shields and mounts read well; the small robed/caped figures still need more silhouette separation.
  • These still frames do not validate acted tint, fog, animations, selection overlays or every map biome.
